About

I am a Consultant Internist and Hepatologist at Sultan Bin Abdulaziz Humanitarian City (SBAHC) in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the College of Medicine, Alfaisal University. I was previously an Assistant Professor at Minia University, Egypt.

My clinical and academic work centres on metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) and the wider field of metabolic liver disease. I lead the Tele-Hepatology Initiative at SBAHC, direct the Lipo-Metabolic Liver Academy, and am a certified Clinical Trial Principal Investigator with the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS).
