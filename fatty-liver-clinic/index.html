MASLD / MASH evaluation

Complete clinical sheet, automatic scores, guideline-based assessment, printable report and a local registry. Works without internet. Every patient has a folder in "Fatty Liver Clinic" on the Desktop, and the registry updates itself.

Sheet order: personal history → complaint → present illness → past, drug and social history → family history → examination → laboratory → imaging → assessment and plan → TrakCare → report.
